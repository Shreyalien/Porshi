info About Porshi (পড়শী)

Porshi (পড়শী) is Bangladesh's dedicated neighborhood directory and mutual-aid network. It empowers citizens to discover verified doorstep technicians, tutors, health practitioners, and emergency hotlines without intermediaries or hidden broker fees. Built on trust, National ID verification, and authentic local recommendations, Porshi connects neighbors to build stronger, self-reliant communities.
